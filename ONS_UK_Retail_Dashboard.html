Key Insight — COVID Impact & Recovery
April 2020 saw the most extreme divergence in ONS RSI history: clothing collapsed −78% MoM as lockdowns shuttered non-essential retail, while online surged +121% YoY. By 2024, a structural plateau settled — online stabilised at ~15% share (vs ~9% pre-COVID), and food inflation drove headline growth while real volumes stagnated.

Methodology
How the forecast is built
Baseline uses a 12-month rolling average with same-month-prior-year as anchor, preserving seasonality. Growth and inflation are applied multiplicatively. Confidence bounds set at ±5% for medium forecast uncertainty.
